# TSE/IFB Quant Rules & Methods Update (2024 – Oct 2026 / Mehr 1405)

Research date: 17 Oct 2026 (25 Mehr 1405). Scope: extends the earlier round (momentum/herding/magnet-effect literature, Moradi 2020, ±3% limit, base volume = 1 share from 22 Dec 2025, T+2, 09:00–12:30 + TAL 12:45–13:00 from 6 Jun 2026, ~1.25% round-trip). Everything below is new or sharpens those items. "In force" vs "announced/planned" is marked explicitly.

## Q1. Rule changes announced or planned Sept–Oct 2026 (Mehr 1405)

### Takeaway
No decision to widen the ±3% band, introduce asymmetric limits, or move to T+1 was found in any Persian or English source through 25 Mehr 1405; the ±3% band was explicitly reconfirmed by the SEO head in Ordibehesht 1405. The concrete Mehr-1405 changes are (a) non-equity ETF trading hours extended to 18:00 from 18/19 Mehr, (b) a planned "queue coefficient" revision to the IFB base-market dynamic base volume, and (c) a wave of supervisory suspensions (6 refineries, manipulation halts) that matter more for a tradability filter than the war-era "42 symbols", of which only 2–3 remained halted by early Shahrivar 1405.

- The engine should treat "limit regime" as a per-instrument-class attribute (3/4/10% and base-market 1/2/3%) with an effective-date table, not a global constant.

## Q2. Recent empirical research on TSE (2023–2026)

### Takeaway
The most relevant new paper is a 1405 Financial Research Journal (University of Tehran) study showing a GA-tuned XGBoost predicts TEDPIX direction with ~84% accuracy and that individual ("real") and institutional ("legal") flow, gold, RSI/volume/MACD and ROA/ROE are the SHAP-dominant features. Internationally, the key design-relevant result is that in emerging limit-constrained markets daily momentum exists but roughly half of its measured magnitude comes from limit-hit observations. No paper using TSETMC order-book data and no formal study of the ±3% regime vs ±5% was found.

### Cited Findings
- Heidari Dalooei, Vahdati, Mohebbi, Bagherpour, "Predictability of TEDPIX using a combined ML approach", Financial Research Journal 28(2), 1405, pp. 464–493, DOI 10.22059/frj.2025.394747.1007738. Data 1398–1403 (English abstract says 2019–2023). XGBoost-GA beats XGBoost, RF, SVM, logistic regression; 84% directional accuracy. SHAP: technical-only model driven by RSI, volume, MACD; combined model adds real and legal liquidity inflows, gold price, ROA, ROE. Authors conclude market is inefficient at weak and semi-strong levels — [jfr.ut.ac.ir article 103114](https://jfr.ut.ac.ir/article_103114.html)
- "Daily Momentum and New Investors in Emerging Stock Markets" (AEA 2025): stocks performing well today continue tomorrow and reverse within a week; return-continuation magnitude falls by more than half when limit-hit observations are excluded, though daily momentum remains significant — [AEA 2025 program](https://www.aeaweb.org/conference/2025/program/paper/dDyQH6K4); [PDF](https://wxiong.mycpanel.princeton.edu/papers/DailyMomentum.pdf)
- China: investors overreact to up-limit events, which erases conventional momentum; a revised momentum measure dropping up-limit days recovers medium-term momentum — [Economic Modelling (ScienceDirect)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0264999322001560); after hitting the 10% upper limit prices rise two more days then reverse — [CUHK CBK](https://cbk.bschool.cuhk.edu.hk/do-daily-price-limits-stabilize-the-markets/); [VoxChina](https://voxchina.org/show-3-49.html)
- Monthly activity reports (گزارش فعالیت ماهانه) carry information: price moves in the direction of news and trading volume rises noticeably around publication — [Journal of Accounting Knowledge, jak.uk.ac.ir](https://jak.uk.ac.ir/article_3125.html?lang=fa)
- Investor reaction to unexpected earnings under market uncertainty uses 3-day CAR around Codal announcement date with TEDPIX as market return — [amf.ui.ac.ir](https://amf.ui.ac.ir/article_23630.html)
- Post-closure June 2025: in the weeks after the 12-day war TEDPIX fell >15% before making a new ATH at the start of 2026 — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/iran-eyes-challenging-stock-market-130306399.html); individuals pulled 64.78 trillion rials in a single day (2 Jul 2025) — [Iran International](https://www.iranintl.com/en/202507022285); ~12 trillion rials retail outflow in first 90 minutes of a session — [Iran Focus](https://iranfocus.com/economy/54630-)

By Shahrivar 1405 the index reached the 6.6m channel — [Nournews](https://nournews.ir/fa/news/343619/), and crossed 8m in Mehr 1405 — [Vananews 18 Mehr](https://vananews.com/fa/news/668016/)
- Exchange rate vs TEDPIX (preprint, 2024): positive long-run relation; ECM adjustment 30.786% per period; no significant interest-rate link — [Preprints.org 202405.1712](https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202405.1712)

- Practitioner flow metrics in use: daily net real-money inflow (~5.8 hemat on a strong day), real buy/sell per-capita ratio 1.5 (one-month high), per-capita buy ~104m vs sell ~108m toman — [YJC](https://www.yjc.ir/fa/news/9138398/); [Eghtesadonline](https://www.eghtesadonline.com/fa/news/2159420/)

### Inferences
- The engine's feature set should include signed real/legal net flow and per-capita ratios (both confirmed as SHAP-important in 1405 TSE research and as the dominant practitioner lens), plus gold, and should test any momentum signal with and without limit-locked days (the AEA paper implies a large share of apparent continuation is mechanical).
- Monthly Codal report dates are a usable event-study anchor (volume and directional price response documented).

- No 2024–2026 gold/dollar–TEDPIX lead-lag paper with a confirmed date found.
- Civilica/SID/Ganj theses were not surfaced by web search.

## Q3. Methodology for limits, queues, halts

### Takeaway
A-share practice offers a direct template: infer limit-hits from prices, forbid fills on limit-locked opens, exclude suspended or limit-locked names from the next-day tradable universe, and assume next-open fills. For validation, purged walk-forward CV with an embargo at least as long as the label horizon is the standard; a 2026 paper shows RankIC collapsing from 0.072 to 0.010 out-of-sample even with correct embargoing. Fill-probability literature (survival analysis, queue-reactive models) is available but calibrated on FX/US books, not TSE queues.

### Cited Findings
- Open-source A-share backtester models next-open fills, trading costs, T+1 and look-ahead checks, and refuses fills at limit-up/limit-down opens — [quant-backtest (GitHub)](https://github.com/HongruiLi5j/quant-backtest)
- Tradability filter example: exclude stocks suspended or hitting a limit on the next trading day; select only tradable, non-limit-up names; exclude new listings pinned at consecutive up-limits for 120 days — [MLF_2021_Final_Project (GitHub)](https://github.com/Harveydentsun/MLF_2021_Final_Project)
- Block buys at limit-up and sells at limit-down; board-specific limits (±10% main, ±20% ChiNext/STAR, ±30% BSE) — [Waylandz US–China differences](https://waylandz.com/quant-book-en/US-China-Market-Differences/)
- Liquidity vanishes right after a limit hit, so backtests show fills that would not occur live — [Gank Interview](https://www.gankinterview.cn/en/blog/a-shares-vs-us-stocks-interviewing-at-top-domestic-private-funds-high-flyerjiuku)
- Limit hits are not flagged in price records and must be inferred from prices; tick size makes this feasible — [arXiv 1503.03548](https://arxiv.org/html/1503.03548v1); statistical properties and pre-hit dynamics of limit hits — [PLOS ONE](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371%2Fjournal.pone.0120312)
- Excluding limit-hit observations halves measured daily-momentum continuation — [AEA 2025](https://www.aeaweb.org/conference/2025/program/paper/dDyQH6K4)
- Purged CV removes training observations whose label windows overlap the test set and adds an embargo buffer — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purged_cross-validation); scikit-learn-compatible implementation with purging, embargo, combinatorial purged CV and deflated Sharpe, with caveat that these do not guarantee independence — [purged-cross-validation (GitHub)](https://github.com/eslazarev/purged-cross-validation)
- "When Alpha Breaks" (arXiv 2603.13252, 2026): LightGBM ranker, 7 PIT-safe features (mom 1/3/12m, vol 20/60d, adv 20d, cross-sectional rank), labels = excess return vs QQQ at 20/60/90d, expanding walk-forward over 109 folds (Feb 2016–Feb 2025), 90-trading-day embargo between latest training label maturity and first prediction date, overlapping labels purged; per-date Spearman RankIC as primary metric; 20d RankIC mean 0.072 (DEV 2016–2023) vs 0.010 (FINAL 2024+); 60d/90d FINAL RankIC −0.005/−0.021; shadow portfolio long top-10/short bottom-10, 20-day rebalances, 10 bps turnover-based cost; uncertainty gate (EWMA of matured RankIC + drift + cross-model disagreement) with 80% precision / 64% recall / 47% abstention — [arXiv 2603.13252](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.13252). Note: the paper has internal inconsistencies (FINAL Sharpe 2.337 in Table 1 vs 1.91 in text).
- Fill probability: queue-reactive model with state-dependent arrivals/cancellations yields semi-analytical fill probabilities at best quotes and one level deeper — [Lokin & Yu, arXiv 2403.02572](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.02572v2); survival-analysis + deep encoder for fill probability with right-censoring (Quantitative Finance 2024) — [Arroyo et al.](https://ideas.repec.org/a/taf/quantf/v24y2024i1p35-57.html); KANFormer survival approach — [arXiv 2512.05734](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.05734); Cont–Stoikov–Talreja Poisson queues are easy to estimate from book data — [TU Delft thesis](https://repository.tudelft.nl/file/File_24c47635-11c6-4af4-995f-eff755b898a1?preview=1); exponential-in-depth fill approximation vs power-law tails — [Toronto lecture notes](https://www.utstat.toronto.edu/sjaimung/courses/sta4505/notes/2014/ExecutionLimitOrders.pdf)
- Empirical lock frequency on IFB base market under the old fixed base volume: 17.2% of symbol-days locked, 2.3% under dynamic model — [Vananews](https://vananews.com/fa/news/666470/)
- Covariance: Ledoit–Wolf shrinks sample covariance toward a structured target (constant variance, single factor, constant correlation) — [PyPortfolioOpt docs](https://pyportfolioopt.readthedocs.io/en/latest/RiskModels.html); nonlinear shrinkage — [Ledoit & Wolf, Markowitz meets Goldilocks](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/323474297); covariance estimation with missing observations (Lounici 2014) cited in — [arXiv 2606.27462](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.27462); a practical rule keeps stocks with ≤32 missing days in the window and sets remaining missing returns to 0 — [arXiv 2210.14854](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2210.14854)

### Inferences
- For TSE: infer locked days as close == ceiling/floor with queue present (TSETMC exposes queue volumes, so inference can be stronger than China's price-only method); a tradability filter should drop names locked or halted at the signal date and model entries at next-day open/auction price with a fill probability conditioned on queue size relative to recent traded volume (the IFB "queue coefficient" definition—traded volume / queue volume—is a ready-made feature).
- Use an embargo ≥ label horizon (e.g. 20 trading days for 1-month labels) plus purging; report DEV vs FINAL RankIC separately and expect large decay.
- For halted names (weeks–months), treat returns as missing rather than zero when estimating covariance, use shrinkage, and record the reopening jump as one observation; the China "set to 0" shortcut understates risk for TSE halts that end with ±20–40% auction gaps.

### Gaps
- No source found on de-smoothing (e.g. Getmansky–Lo–Makarov-type autocorrelation unsmoothing) applied to halted/stale equity series; this remains an unsourced recommendation.
- No published TSE-specific queue fill-probability calibration.
- López de Prado's own texts were not fetched; purged CV facts come from secondary implementations.

## Q4. Factor-model / ML practice for small emerging markets

### Takeaway
Practitioner consensus for small cross-sections is a shallow gradient-boosted ranker (depth 3–4, ≤15 leaves, heavy subsampling) on percentile-rank labels, evaluated with per-date Spearman RankIC (Newey–West t-stats, FDR control), quintile/decile long–short with turnover-based costs, under purged walk-forward CV. ONNX export via onnxmltools.convert_lightgbm → ML.NET `ApplyOnnxModel` is documented end-to-end except for the ZipMap output quirk. 2025–2026 comparative studies find transformers/LSTMs not uniformly better than ARIMA/RF out-of-sample and no meta-analysis exists.

### Cited Findings
- Cross-sectional alpha repo: eight signals on 495 S&P 500 names, Newey–West ICs, Benjamini–Hochberg FDR, cost-aware quintiles, shallow LightGBM (depth 4, 15 leaves, heavy subsampling) regressing cross-sectional percentile rank of next-month return under purged walk-forward CV; naive z-score averaging lost 7.8%/yr — [equity-xs-alpha (GitHub)](https://github.com/tejaspandya9598/equity-xs-alpha)
- LightGBM LambdaMART ranker under purged/embargoed CV with 21-day horizon for Indian equities — [cross-sectional-ranking-engine-3 (GitHub)](https://github.com/ZethetaIntern/cross-sectional-ranking-engine-3)
- Full evaluation protocol with RankIC, shadow portfolio, 10 bps turnover costs, volatility sizing w = s·min(1, c_vol/√vol_20d), gating — [arXiv 2603.13252](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.13252)
- ONNX conversion: train LightGBM, export booster, `convert_lightgbm` from onnxmltools with FloatTensorType input and target opset (9 or 13); pinned versions onnxmltools==1.7.0, lightgbm==3.2.1, onnx==1.17.0 — [Microsoft Fabric ONNX overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/fabric/data-science/onnx-overview); [SynapseML quickstart](https://microsoft.github.io/SynapseML/docs/Explore%20Algorithms/Deep%20Learning/Quickstart%20-%20ONNX%20Model%20Inference/)
- ML.NET: `ApplyOnnxModel(outputColumnNames, inputColumnNames, modelFile, shapeDictionary, gpuDeviceId, fallbackToCpu)` in Microsoft.ML.OnnxTransformer; output column names must match ONNX graph outputs — [Microsoft Learn API](https://learn.microsoft.com/dotnet/api/microsoft.ml.onnxcatalog.applyonnxmodel); [sample](https://source.dot.net/Microsoft.ML.Samples/Dynamic/Transforms/ApplyOnnxModel.cs.html); [AutoML ONNX in .NET](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/machine-learning/how-to-use-automl-onnx-model-dotnet)
- Deep-learning evidence: six US equities 2014–2024, walk-forward OOS: ARIMA and Random Forest remain hard to beat, transformer competitive but not dominant, gains depend on evaluation design — [JRFM 19(3):203](https://www.mdpi.com/1911-8074/19/3/203); LSTM/Transformer/econometric comparison: transformers best overall but accuracy degrades by 5-day horizon — [ScienceDirect S2666827025001136](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666827025001136); in HFT LOB data LSTM beats Transformer at all horizons due to noise — [arXiv 2302.13850](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2302.13850); a single-stock LSTM+news study claims strong OOS — [Journal of Economic Analysis 2025](https://www.anserpress.org/journal/jea/display/428/jea-00109.pdf)

### Inferences
- For a TSE universe of a few hundred liquid names, a regression-on-rank LightGBM (not LambdaMART) with ≤7–15 features and strong regularisation is the defensible default; deep sequence models have no supportive evidence at monthly horizons.
- Serving path: export with ZipMap disabled so probabilities/scores are a plain float tensor; inspect graph I/O names (Netron) before wiring `ApplyOnnxModel`.

### Gaps
- No source reporting decile spreads or turnover figures for a purged-CV LightGBM ranker in an emerging market.
- No 2024–2026 meta-analysis of transformer/LSTM return forecasting was found; only individual comparative studies.
- ZipMap/sequence-of-maps handling in ML.NET not documented in retrieved sources.

## Q5. Persian financial NLP / LLM state 2025–2026

### Takeaway
On the broadest Persian benchmark (MELAC, 41 models, 19 datasets) closed models lead (GPT-4o 72.6, Gemini 2.0 Flash 70.7) and the best open models are multilingual (Qwen3-32B 60.3, Gemma-3-27B 60.3, Aya-Expanse-32B 59.6); Persian-specific fine-tunes trail (Hormoz-8B 50.5 ≈ Llama-3.1-8B 49.5; Dorna2 47.7 below its base). No Persian financial benchmark or Codal-extraction study exists; the English FinTagging/FLAME frameworks are the nearest templates.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- Earnings models for exporters should use the Exchange-Centre remittance rate (lagged by the IME weekly base-price cycle, ~1–2 weeks) for revenue, not the free rate; the free–official gap (~46%) is itself a regime variable.
- Real-return computation must state the deflator: SCI nationwide CPI vs CBI urban CPI differ by ~6 pp on p2p inflation; a nominal risk-free of 35–40% implies real rates near −30% to −50%, so Sharpe-type metrics against the fund yield will flatter equity strategies; prefer reporting returns relative to gold-fund and dollar benchmarks as well.
- TEDPIX's nominal rise from ~4m (May 2026) to ~8.4m (Mehr 1405) is of the same order as cumulative inflation plus the dollar move; nominal momentum/trend signals need deflation.

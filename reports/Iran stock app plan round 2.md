# Second-round research reshapes the Boors plan

The October 2026 deep-dive confirms almost every structural choice in the Boors development plan and overturns exactly one: TradingView's Advanced Charts library cannot be used, because its Free Advanced Charts Agreement (v.0626.FAC, June 2026) restricts the licence to companies running public, free, non-open-source implementations and explicitly excludes "private, personal or internal uses" ([TradingView licence PDF](https://s3.amazonaws.com/tradingview/charting_library_license_agreement.pdf)), so open decision 3 in the plan is closed in favour of Lightweight Charts v5.2.1 (Apache-2.0 with a mandatory attribution link). Everything else in the stack is current and licence-clean as of 17 October 2026: .NET 10 LTS (supported to November 2028), bit BlazorUI and Bit.Boilerplate 10.6.2 (MIT), Apache ECharts 6.1.0, PostgreSQL 18 with TimescaleDB Community under the Tiger Data licence, Quartz.NET 4.5.1, Microsoft.Extensions.Http.Resilience 10.11.0, DuckDB.NET 1.5.6.1, ONNX Runtime 1.31.0 (while ML.NET is stalled in 6.0 previews), and Microsoft Agent Framework 1.0 GA replacing Semantic Kernel. The Iran-specific findings sharpen the hybrid topology rather than change it: Iran-hosted servers cannot reach `api.telegram.org`, Bale's Bot API is a documented Telegram clone at `https://tapi.bale.ai/bot<token>/`, ParsPack's `mirror.abrha.net` solves NuGet/Docker access from Iranian IPs, OpenAI and Anthropic exclude Iran so LLM calls from the Iran node must go through a Toman-billed gateway such as AvalAI, and FCM push has been broken in Iran since 2022. The data layer is now specified to the field level: Codal's `categories` endpoint gives LetterType IDs (58 monthly activity, 6 financial statements, 11 material disclosures, 20 AGM decisions, 30 DPS schedule), the monthly report embeds a parseable `var datasource` JSON, TSETMC's filter language is defined on four official help pages, `GetMarketWatch` returns compact keys that map onto the filter variables, and `yVal`/`flow`/`cgrValCot` codes were verified live. On the rules side, the ±3% band was reconfirmed in Ordibehesht 1405, non-equity ETF sessions now run to 18:00, limits differ by instrument class (3/4/10% and 1/2/3% on the IFB base market), the IFB base market still uses a dynamic base volume despite the "one share" rule elsewhere, and fee totals conflict (1.25% vs 1.43%) so brokerage must be a parameter. The quant literature adds a 1405 XGBoost/SHAP paper ranking real/legal flow among the top TEDPIX predictors, the A-share playbook for limit-hit handling, purged cross-validation with an embargo at least as long as the label horizon, and the MELAC benchmark showing multilingual open models (Qwen3-32B, Gemma-3-27B) beat Persian fine-tunes such as Dorna2, which the plan currently names as its local fallback. The final section lists 38 concrete edits to the plan, keyed to its section numbers.

## Stack validated with versions; one licence blocker removed

The runtime choice holds. .NET 10 reached GA on 11 November 2025 as an LTS release with three years of patches, and .NET 8 and 9 both reach end of life on 10 November 2026, so a project starting now has no sensible alternative ([heise](https://www.heise.de/en/news/NET-10-0-is-Ready-11075047.html?seite=3)). Blazor in .NET 10 gained circuit-state persistence across lost connections, declarative `[PersistentState]`, a `ResourcePreloader` component, inlined boot config, HttpClient response streaming by default and a PWA template that registers the service worker with `updateViaCache: 'none'` ([Microsoft Learn: ASP.NET Core 10 release notes](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/aspnet/core/release-notes/aspnetcore-10.0)). .NET 11 is an STS release expected on 10 November 2026; it previews a CoreCLR-based WASM runtime with multithreading that only becomes stable in .NET 12, plus an Aspire-hosted Blazor gateway and token refresh for long-lived SignalR connections ([Iron Software news](https://ironsoftware.com/news/industry-news/dotnet-11-aspnet-core-blazor-agentic-web/)). The practical consequence for Boors is that **multithreaded WASM is a 2027 story**: CPU-heavy indicator and screener work belongs on the server or inside the JS chart libraries, not in Web Workers. For the web render mode (plan open decision 4), WebAssembly (standalone or Auto) is the better fit, because a Blazor Server circuit would route every five-second market diff through the server-side render tree, while WASM clients receive compact SignalR diffs and render locally. One tooling caveat is worth recording in the risks table: heise's review calls Blazor Hot Reload in .NET 10 "still a major disappointment because it often crashes", and .NET MAUI still has no Linux target ([heise](https://www.heise.de/en/news/NET-10-0-is-Ready-11075047.html?seite=3)).

The UI kit decision is confirmed and strengthened. `Bit.BlazorUI` 10.6.2 shipped on 22 September 2026 under MIT, targets net8.0 through net10.0 (including net10.0-browser), and prereleases 11.0.0-pre-10 and 10.7.0-pre-02 landed on 13 and 16 October 2026, so the project is actively maintained ([NuGet: Bit.BlazorUI](https://www.nuget.org/packages/Bit.BlazorUI/)). `Bit.Boilerplate` 10.6.2 installs with `dotnet new install Bit.Boilerplate@10.6.2` and advertises identity with passwordless sign-in and 2FA, offline database and sync, PWA via Bswup, OpenTelemetry, an AI chatbot with RAG and .NET Aspire in a single codebase for web, Android, iOS, Windows and macOS ([NuGet: Bit.Boilerplate](https://www.nuget.org/packages/Bit.Boilerplate); [GitHub: bitfoundation/bitplatform](https://github.com/bitfoundation/bitplatform)). Two component facts matter for Boors specifically: `BitDatePicker` renders the Persian calendar natively through its `Culture` parameter, with demos showing "مهر 1405" labels and both Farsi and "Fingilish" month names ([bit BlazorUI docs: DatePicker](https://blazorui.bitplatform.dev/components/datepicker)), and `BitDataGrid` offers row virtualisation, column virtualisation with pixel widths, a server-side `OnRead` callback, infinite scroll and `Dir="BitDir.Rtl"` that makes frozen columns and keyboard navigation follow reading direction ([bit BlazorUI docs: DataGrid](https://blazorui.bitplatform.dev/components/datagrid)). No competitor matches that combination: MudBlazor 9.x needs the third-party MudBlazorJalali (whose 7.6.1 version tracks MudBlazor 7) ([NuGet: MudBlazorJalali](https://www.nuget.org/packages/mudblazorjalali)), Syncfusion's Community Licence lapses above $1M revenue or five developers and does not document a Persian calendar ([Syncfusion Blazor components](https://www.syncfusion.com/blazor-components)), Telerik costs roughly $1,028–$1,503 per developer ([ComponentSource](https://www.componentsource.com/product/telerik-ui-blazor/prices?c=usd)), and Fluent UI Blazor v5 was still a release candidate in April 2026 ([NuGet profile: fluentui-blazor](https://feed.nuget.org/profiles/fluentui-blazor)). The one honest risk is community size: bit has 1.4k stars and 720K downloads against MudBlazor's tens of millions, so the MIT licence and the ability to fork are the mitigation.

For the live grid, the 3,100-row market watch refreshing every five seconds is modest once virtualised, because only 30–60 DOM rows exist and the cost is dominated by diffing visible rows. Microsoft's QuickGrid documentation (updated 8 October 2026) makes the in-box grid a "reference architecture and performance baseline" and says there are "no current plans to extend QuickGrid with features that full-blown commercial grids tend to offer" ([Microsoft Learn: QuickGrid](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/aspnet/core/blazor/components/quickgrid)), which leaves BitDataGrid as the natural pick alongside bit BlazorUI. No Blazor grid documents cell-level patching, and the AG Grid Blazor wrappers are immature (AgGrid.Blazor 0.9.0 exposes "a relatively small subset", and the MeshWeaver wrapper uses AG Grid Enterprise, which needs a commercial licence) ([NuGet: AgGrid.Blazor](https://www.nuget.org/packages/AgGrid.Blazor); [NuGet: MeshWeaver.Blazor.AgGrid](https://www-0.nuget.org/packages/MeshWeaver.Blazor.AgGrid/2.0.0-Preview2)). The plan's phrase "cell-level patching from SignalR diffs" (§5.3) therefore describes an app-level pattern that must be built: mutate row objects in place, batch to one `StateHasChanged` per tick, and drive flash animations with CSS classes keyed on a change timestamp. A spike at the "3,000 rows, 5-second updates" profile belongs in Phase 3, since no independent benchmark exists.

### Charting: Lightweight Charts v5 in, Advanced Charts out

Lightweight Charts is exactly where the plan wants it. v5.2.1 (12 August 2026) is the latest release; v5.0.8 added `addPane` and relative pane sizes, v5.0.4 price-positioned markers, v5.0.8 formatters that receive all tick values at once, v5.2.1 crosshair performance with many markers plus an accessibility plugin, and the first official plugin package set (ten plugins and a scaffolding tool) landed on 16 September 2026 ([GitHub releases](https://github.com/tradingview/lightweight-charts/releases)). The v5 bundle is about 35 KB ([TradingView blog](https://www.tradingview.com/blog/en/tradingview-lightweight-charts-version-5-50837)). The licence is Apache-2.0 with a twist the plan must honour: "You shall add the 'attribution notice' from the NOTICE file and a link to https://www.tradingview.com/ to the page of your website or mobile application", satisfiable through the `attributionLogo` chart option ([GitHub: tradingview/lightweight-charts](https://github.com/tradingview/lightweight-charts)). Jalali axis labels are feasible because `tickMarkFormatter`, `timeFormatter` and `priceFormatter` give full control of label text, but there is no RTL or mirrored time-axis option ([Lightweight Charts docs: TimeScaleOptions](https://tradingview.github.io/lightweight-charts/docs/api/interfaces/TimeScaleOptions); [LocalizationOptions](https://tradingview.github.io/lightweight-charts/docs/api/interfaces/LocalizationOptions)), which matches the plan's "time axes still run left to right" rule.

Advanced Charts is a different animal. TradingView states it does not provide Advanced Charts or the Trading Platform "for personal use, hobbies, studies, or testing" and that the licences "are only available to companies for use in public web projects" ([TradingView free charting libraries](https://www.tradingview.com/free-charting-libraries/)). The agreement's §2.4 restricts use to "a public access service (as a free offering only...)" and not "private, personal or internal uses"; §2.5 forbids hosting the library in any public repository or making a project using it open source; §2.11 requires marketing backlinks and a blog post announcing the partnership; the client must also grant TradingView "free, unlimited access" to the product ([TradingView licence PDF](https://s3.amazonaws.com/tradingview/charting_library_license_agreement.pdf)). Each of those conflicts with a personal-first app that may later charge a subscription. **Remove Advanced Charts from the architecture and the open-decisions list.** For treemaps, heatmaps, calendars and matrices, Apache ECharts 6.1.0 (19 May 2026) adds a headless matrix coordinate system, `dataMin`/`dataMax` in nice axis extents and a `cursor` option for candlestick series ([GitHub: apache/echarts releases](https://github.com/apache/echarts/releases)), and ECharts 6.0 introduced a new default theme, dark-mode support and the matrix coordinate system that suits sector heatmaps ([ECharts handbook v6](https://echarts.apache.org/handbook/en/basics/release-note/v6-feature)). Commercial WebGL charting is not needed at this data volume; SciChart.js would cost $1,299–$1,699 per developer per year with a 15,000-end-user cap on the standard licence ([SciChart pricing guide](https://www.scichart.com/scichart-pricing-licensing-guide/)).

### Data layer, resilience, scheduling, AI runtime

PostgreSQL 18.6 and 17.11 are the current minors and PostgreSQL 19 RC1 shipped on 15 October 2026 ([postgresql.org release notes](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/release/)), so the plan's "PostgreSQL 17" should become 18. Timescale, Inc. now does business as Tiger Data; the Timescale License effective 17 June 2025 keeps Community Edition features free for internal use and for "Value Added Products or Services" that are not primarily a database, add substantial value of a different nature, and prohibit users from changing the schema, while forbidding DBaaS-style resale ([Tiger Data legal: licenses](https://www.tigerdata.com/legal/licenses); [Tiger Data docs: editions](https://www.tigerdata.com/docs/about/latest/timescaledb-editions)). Boors is a value-added product, so compression and continuous aggregates are usable in both the personal and a later multi-tenant phase, with the single caution that tenants must never get raw schema or SQL access. Npgsql.EntityFrameworkCore.PostgreSQL 10.0.3 fully supports EF Core 10's JSON complex types mapped to `jsonb` ([Npgsql EF Core 10.0 release notes](https://www.npgsql.org/efcore/release-notes/10.0.html)), and EF Core 10 (LTS to 10 November 2028) adds `LeftJoin`/`RightJoin`, named query filters such as `HasQueryFilter("SoftDeletionFilter", ...)`, lambda `ExecuteUpdate` and parameterised-collection padding ([Microsoft Learn: What's new in EF Core 10](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/ef/core/what-is-new/ef-core-10.0/whatsnew)); named query filters are the natural mechanism for the plan's tenant isolation. On the local side, DuckDB.NET.Data.Full 1.5.6.1 (15 October 2026, MIT, DuckDB 1.5.6) is current with net8.0/net10.0 builds, though mobile TFMs appear only as computed, so do not assume Android/iOS support ([NuGet: DuckDB.NET.Data.Full](https://www.nuget.org/packages/DuckDB.NET.Data.Full)); Parquet.Net 6.1.0 (11 August 2026) is the fully managed writer ([NuGet: Parquet.Net](https://www.nuget.org/packages/Parquet.Net)); LiteDB should be avoided because its stable 5.0.21 dates from July 2024 and 6.0 is prerelease ([NuGet: LiteDB](https://www.nuget.org/packages/LiteDB)).

For resilience, `Microsoft.Extensions.Http.Resilience` 10.11.0 (14 October 2026, MIT, built on Polly v8) is the default, and `Microsoft.Extensions.Http.Polly` is formally deprecated in its favour ([NuGet: Microsoft.Extensions.Http.Resilience](https://www.nuget.org/packages/Microsoft.Extensions.Http.Resilience); [NuGet mirror: Microsoft.Extensions.Http.Polly](https://nuget.rza.ru/packages/microsoft.extensions.http.polly/10.0.8)). For scheduling, Quartz 4.5.1 (15 October 2026, Apache-2.0, ~190M downloads, .NET 10) is the safe choice ([NuGet: Quartz](https://www.nuget.org/packages/Quartz)). TickerQ 10.4.0 (12 May 2026, MIT or Apache-2.0, EF Core storage for PostgreSQL, built-in dashboard with "no paid add-ons") is attractive but young, with only vendor benchmarks ([NuGet: TickerQ](https://www.nuget.org/packages/TickerQ)). Hangfire's Core is LGPL-3.0 and its Redis storage sits in paid tiers of $500–$4,500 per organisation per year ([Hangfire pricing](https://www.hangfire.io/pricing/)), so it stays off the list. Aspire (renamed from ".NET Aspire") is at 13.6 (29 September 2026) with persistent dashboard storage, Blazor WebAssembly debugging commands, Docker Compose/Kubernetes publishing and first-party Java/Rust hosting ([aspire.dev what's new 13.6](https://aspire.dev/whats-new/aspire-13-6/)), which suits local orchestration of Postgres+Timescale, MinIO, Redis and Ollama.

The ML and LLM runtime picture changed materially since the first round. Microsoft.ML.OnnxRuntime 1.31.0 (9 October 2026) is current and explicitly supports models from scikit-learn, LightGBM and XGBoost ([NuGet: Microsoft.ML.OnnxRuntime](https://www.nuget.org/packages/Microsoft.ML.OnnxRuntime); [NuGet: Microsoft.ML.OnnxRuntime.Managed](https://www.nuget.org/packages/microsoft.ml.onnxruntime.managed)), whereas ML.NET's newest package is 6.0.0-preview.26160.2 from 12 March 2026 with every package prerelease ([NuGet profile: mlnet](https://www.nuget.org/profiles/mlnet)). The plan's "ONNX Runtime (models trained in Python LightGBM)" is therefore exactly right, and ML.NET should stay out of the critical path. For LLM plumbing, Microsoft.Extensions.AI is the base `IChatClient` abstraction ([.NET blog, 28 Jan 2026](https://devblogs.microsoft.com/dotnet/dotnet-ai-essentials-the-core-building-blocks-explained/)), Microsoft Agent Framework reached 1.0 GA on 3 April 2026 with Microsoft.Agents.AI at 1.25.0 on 14 October 2026 ([NuGet: Microsoft.Agents.AI](https://www.nuget.org/packages/Microsoft.Agents.AI); [Start Debugging](https://startdebugging.net/2026/06/microsoft-agent-framework-vs-semantic-kernel-for-a-greenfield-net-agent/)), and Semantic Kernel is on a "legacy track: critical bugs and security fixes only" ([Managed Code blog](https://managed-code.com/blog-post/semantic-kernel-is-done)). OllamaSharp 5.4.25 implements the MEAI `IChatClient` and `IEmbeddingGenerator` interfaces ([NuGet: OllamaSharp](https://www.nuget.org/packages/OllamaSharp)). For bots, Telegram.Bot 22.10.3.3 (9 October 2026, MIT, Bot API 10.3) is current ([NuGet: Telegram.Bot](https://www.nuget.org/packages/Telegram.Bot)), and three dedicated Bale packages exist (HexaBale 1.3.0, BaleBotNet 5.0.0, BaleSharp 2.1.1) ([NuGet: HexaBale](https://www.nuget.org/packages/hexabale); [NuGet: BaleBotNet](https://www.nuget.org/packages/BaleBotNet/5.0.0); [NuGet: BaleSharp](https://www.nuget.org/packages/BaleSharp)). For Persian utilities, DNTPersianUtils.Core 7.0.2 (26 September 2026, Apache-2.0) covers Jalali formatting, Persian digits, text normalisation of Arabic ي/ك, official holidays and business-day calculations ([NuGet: DNTPersianUtils.Core](https://www.nuget.org/packages/DNTPersianUtils.Core)), which overlaps with the plan's §3.5 normalisation and §2.12 holiday table. Vazirmatn v33.003 is OFL-1.1 with a variable font ([GitHub: rastikerdar/vazirmatn](https://github.com/rastikerdar/vazirmatn)). On the JS side, the Day.js Jalali plugins are stale (jalali-plugin-dayjs last updated August 2024, forked from an unmaintained upstream) ([jalali-plugin-dayjs README](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/jalali-plugin-dayjs@1.1.4/README.md)), so the plan's `Intl.DateTimeFormat('fa-IR-u-ca-persian')` approach is the right one.

For the desktop and mobile clients, .NET MAUI 10's stated focus is "to improve product quality"; it adds `WebResourceRequested` interception for BlazorWebView (useful for serving cached chart assets offline), an Aspire service-defaults template and Android API 36 defaults, but still no Linux ([Microsoft Learn: What's new in .NET MAUI for .NET 10](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/maui/whats-new/dotnet-10)). Photino.Blazor last shipped 4.0.13 in January 2025 for .NET 8/9 ([NuGet: Photino.Blazor](https://www.nuget.org/packages/Photino.Blazor)) and Electron.NET is stale at 23.6.2 from February 2024 ([NuGet: ElectronNET.API](https://www.nuget.org/packages/ElectronNET.API)), so MAUI Blazor Hybrid remains the only supported path and bit Boilerplate targets it directly.

## Iran infrastructure: toman prices, mirrors, and a Telegram wall

Domestic hosting is priced in toman and usable for a .NET plus Postgres stack. Liara's PaaS and managed-database tiers run from Earth at 750,000 T/month (0.5 GB RAM) through Saturn at 4,125,000 T (4 GB RAM, 2 vCPU) to Pluto at 22,000,000 T (32 GB, 16 vCPU), with PostgreSQL among the engines and S3-compatible object storage at 475,000 T/month for 20 GB; all plans sit in Iran on 10 Gbit shared bandwidth ([Liara pricing](https://liara.ir/pricing/)). Hamravesh's Darkube prices per resource: 595,000 T/month per vCore and 290,000 T per GB RAM in the Iran data centre, 990,000 T and 480,000 T in its Germany region, external traffic at 500 T/GB and a dedicated external IP at 4,800,000 T/year ([Hamravesh pricing](https://hamravesh.com/pricing)). A 4 GB/2 vCPU app plus a similar managed Postgres on Liara is therefore roughly 8–9 million T/month at list price, and Hamravesh's Germany region lets one vendor bill both nodes in toman, at the cost of a sanctioned counterparty for the abroad node. ArvanCloud has offered managed databases since spring 2024 and geo-redundant backups since Shahrivar 1404 ([Digiato](https://digiato.com/iran-technology-news/arvancloud-managed-cloud-database); [Zoomit](https://www.zoomit.ir/tech-iran/447536-arvancloud-new-cloud-backup-feature-business-continuity/)), but no official price table could be fetched.

The build-chain problem the plan anticipated in §5.1 now has a concrete solution. ParsPack announced developer mirrors on 27 April 2026 (7 Ordibehesht 1405) for Docker, npm, PyPI, Composer, NuGet, Maven, Go and Ubuntu/Debian, explicitly "to neutralise IP-based sanctions" and keep CI/CD alive during outages ([Digiato](https://digiato.com/tech/parspack-developer-repositories-mirror)). The NuGet endpoint is `https://mirror.abrha.net/repository/nuget/index.json` (add with `dotnet nuget add source ... --name ParspackMirror`), npm is `https://mirror.abrha.net/repository/npm/`, and PyPI is `https://mirror.abrha.net/repository/pypi/simple` ([Zoomit](https://www.zoomit.ir/computer-learning/459207-bypass-internet-outage-developer-mirrors/)); ParsPack's docs list separate Docker Registry and Docker Engine mirror pages and say mirrors are served at half-price traffic ([ParsPack docs](https://docs.parspack.com/reference/mirror/)). Docker Hub itself is "officially blocked by Docker because of US laws" for Iranian users, with `registry-mirrors` in the daemon config as the workaround ([Substack](https://ahmadallish.substack.com/p/using-docker-registry-from-iran-65e1a4efffcc)). No official source confirms a hard Microsoft block on `dotnet` SDK downloads from Iranian IPs, and GitHub was on the January 2026 whitelist ([Filterwatch](https://filter.watch/english/2026/01/28/network-monitoring-january-2026-from-regional-disuptions-to-total-blackout-and-whitelisted-access/)), so the correct posture is to pin NuGet to the mirror with nuget.org as fallback and vendor the SDK installer, while keeping CI abroad as the plan already says.

The reliability picture is sobering but consistent with the plan's assumptions. Iran's internet went dark on 8 January 2026, relaxed to a whitelist on 28 January, dropped to 4% connectivity after the 28 February strikes and about 1% through March and April, and was only partially restored on 26 May; the minister put the cost at $35.7M per day, and the TSE index lost 450,000 points over four days ([Wikipedia: 2026 Internet blackout in Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Internet_blackout_in_Iran)). Even the domestic network was "largely inaccessible" on 8 January, and traffic ran at about 25% of pre-shutdown levels by late January ([Filterwatch](https://filter.watch/english/2026/01/28/network-monitoring-january-2026-from-regional-disuptions-to-total-blackout-and-whitelisted-access/)). As of 28 September 2026 international connectivity was "up nationally" but Cloudflare measured a median download of 4.7 Mbps and 135 ms latency for 12 August to 28 September ([Nabz-e Gheymat](https://nabzgheymat.ir/%D8%A2%D8%AE%D8%B1%DB%8C%D9%86-%D8%A7%D8%AE%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D8%A7%DB%8C%D9%86%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%86%D8%AA-%D8%A7%DB%8C%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%86-06-07-1405/)). On 17 October 2026 the SCC secretary declassified the 32-article resolution behind the December 2024 unblocking of Google Play and WhatsApp, while confirming that Telegram and YouTube remain blocked and WhatsApp was re-restricted ([Khabar Foori](https://www.khabarfoori.com/%D8%A8%D8%AE%D8%B4-%D8%A7%D9%82%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%AF%DB%8C-145/3252936-%D9%85%D8%B5%D9%88%D8%A8%D9%87-%D9%85%D8%AD%D8%B1%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%87-%D9%81%DB%8C%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B1%DB%8C%D9%86%DA%AF-%D8%A7%D8%AF%D8%A7%D9%85%D9%87-%D8%AF%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%84%DB%8C%D9%86%DA%A9-%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%AF-%D9%BE%D8%B4%D8%AA-%D8%AF%D8%B1%D9%87%D8%A7%DB%8C-%D8%A8%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%87-%D9%85%D8%AD%D8%AF%D9%88%D8%AF%DB%8C%D8%AA-%D8%A7%DB%8C%D9%86%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%86%D8%AA-%DA%A9%DB%8C%D8%B3%D8%AA)). Legal whitelisted access for businesses exists only as "Internet Pro"/"Pro SIM", a private mobile APN with packages around 2,000,000 T for 40–50 GB, international traffic up to about 40,000 T/GB, daily caps of 2–5 GB, eligibility limited to registered companies, chamber members and researchers, and an explicit note that it is "not a general solution for filtered sites" ([Eghtesad Online](https://www.eghtesadonline.com/fa/news/2133662/%D8%B5%D9%81%D8%B1-%D8%AA%D8%A7-%D8%B5%D8%AF-%D8%B3%DB%8C%D9%85%E2%80%8C%DA%A9%D8%A7%D8%B1%D8%AA-%D9%BE%D8%B1%D9%88-%D8%A7%DB%8C%D9%86%D8%AA%D8%B1%D9%86%D8%AA-%D9%BE%D8%B1%D9%88-%D8%A8%D8%B1%D8%A7%DB%8C-%DA%86%D9%87-%DA%A9%D8%B3%D8%A7%D9%86%DB%8C-%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA-%D9%82%DB%8C%D9%85%D8%AA-%D9%88-%D9%86%D8%AD%D9%88%D9%87-%D8%AB%D8%A8%D8%AA%E2%80%8C%D9%86%D8%A7%D9%85)), while the ICT minister insists "tiered internet or a 'whitelist' system has no validity" ([Wikipedia: Sattar Hashemi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sattar_Hashemi)). None of this is a data-centre-grade egress product, so the plan's assumption that the Iran node must run autonomously for weeks is correct, and the sync design should be outbound-from-Iran over plain TLS to a non-blocked domain with queue and retry.

Two reachability facts change bot and alert design. First, apps hosted inside Iran "cannot send requests to the Telegram Bot API and cannot receive webhooks"; the documented workaround is a relay abroad with the `api.telegram.org` base URL swapped ([GitHub: telegram-web-bridge](https://github.com/AmirrezaNasiri/telegram-web-bridge)). The plan already runs the Telegram bot on the abroad node, which is now confirmed as mandatory rather than merely prudent. Second, Bale's Bot API is fully documented: base URL `https://tapi.bale.ai/bot<token>/METHOD_NAME`, "based on the Telegram Bot API with minor changes" so Telegram libraries work, `getUpdates` long polling or `setWebhook` on ports 443 and 88 with the last 2,000 updates kept for 24 hours, `sendMessage` up to 4,096 characters, in-app IRR payments through `sendInvoice`/`createInvoiceLink` with a 10-second `answerPreCheckoutQuery` window and the test token `WALLET-TEST-1111111111111111`, interactive rate limits with `retry_after`, and a prepaid Business API at `/business/bot<TOKEN>/` for bulk messages ([docs.bale.ai](https://docs.bale.ai/)). Community libraries note there is no `parse_mode` parameter (always Markdown) ([baleio on PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/baleio/)). Eitaa exposes only a send-oriented API via eitaayar.ir with no public documentation ([eitaayar.ir](https://eitaayar.ir/)), and Rubika has only unofficial libraries, so neither should carry interactive features.

Push and SMS costs are now quantified. Iranian users have been unable to obtain FCM tokens since 3 August 2022, attributed to sanctions ([Digiato](https://digiato.com/article/2022/08/09/firebase-cloud-messaging-banned-in-iran-by-google)), which makes the plan's "web push" channel unreliable for users inside Iran; in-app SignalR, Bale and SMS are the dependable channels there. Operator SMS tariffs for 1405 run 11.6–34.6 T per 70-character part depending on language and SIM type ([Melipayamak blog](https://www.melipayamak.com/blog/posts/sms-pricing/)), and Kavenegar's OTP lookup requires its Advanced plan at 600,000 T/year or Super Advanced at 1,200,000 T/year ([Kavenegar pricing](https://kavenegar.com/pricing.html)). Reverse reachability remains unproven: the SEO head, asked to open Codal and TSETMC to investors abroad, said the matter would be reviewed and an alternative route offered if it were tied to information security ([Nabz-e Bourse](https://nabzebourse.com/fa/news/83105/بورس-تعطیل-یا-محدود-می‌شود)), and banks have practised "Iran Access" blocking of foreign IPs ([Zoomit](https://www.zoomit.ir/tech-iran/402852-iran-access-digital-economy)). The first-round finding that `cdn.tsetmc.com` answered from a US IP stands, but the Iran node should remain the primary collector.

LLM access is the sharpest constraint. OpenAI omits Iran from its supported countries and began blocking API traffic from unsupported regions in July 2024 ([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/openai-to-restrict-api-access-for-unsupported-countries-in-july/)); Anthropic lists Iran on neither its API nor claude.ai country lists and in September 2025 barred companies majority-controlled by entities from Iran ([Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/tech-gadgets/2025/09/05/amazon-backed-anthropic-says-no-to-china-russia-iran-north-korea-on-claude-chatbot/190109)). The legitimate route from inside Iran is a Toman-billed, OpenAI-compatible gateway: AvalAI documents `https://api.avalai.ir/v1` as a single endpoint compatible with the OpenAI SDKs and lists OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, DeepSeek, Qwen, Meta, Mistral and others with prices in USD per million tokens ([docs.avalai.ir](https://docs.avalai.ir/)). The plan's "LLM calls go from the abroad node only" (§6.5) should become "direct vendor APIs from the abroad node; AvalAI from the Iran node when the link is down", with the MEAI `IChatClient` abstraction hiding the difference. Local inference remains expensive: an RTX 4090 was quoted at 285–500 million T in 2025 reports ([Zoomit](https://www.zoomit.ir/graphic/435678-rtx-5090-gpu-iran-market/); [Gadgetnews](https://gadgetnews.net/966727/graphics-card-500-million-iran-market/)), roughly one to two years of mid-tier PaaS.

For the gated public phase, the commercial plumbing is clearer. Zarinpal charges 0.5% capped at 16,000 T plus 500 T per transaction and issues Enamad (the e-trust seal) for 600,000 T ([Zarinpal pricing](https://www.zarinpal.com/pricing)); Zibal charges 1% capped at 20,000 T on successful transactions only ([Zibal pricing](https://zibal.ir/pricing)). Enamad requires domain ownership, an adult applicant and FATA police vetting ([Zarinpal blog](https://www.zarinpal.com/blog/%D8%A7%DB%8C%D9%86%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AF-%DA%86%DB%8C%D8%B3%D8%AA/)). The licensing math favours the type-2 adviser: a private joint-stock company with 50 million T minimum capital for buy/sell/hold recommendations, analysis and education ([Eghtesad Online](https://www.eghtesadonline.com/fa/news/567663/جزییات-دستورالعمل-تاسیس-و-فعالیت-مشاور-سرمایه-گذاری-نوع-دوم)), against 80 billion rials for a general investment adviser and 60 billion rials for a financial-information processor under the 3 Azar 1400 circular ([Eghtesad Online](https://www.eghtesadonline.com/fa/news/583819/حداقل-سرمایه-لازم-برای-نهادهای-مالی-ابلاغ-شد-جزییات)). ## Data specs reach field level: Codal, filters, market watch, funds, macro

### Codal: the categories endpoint is the taxonomy the plan needed

The first-round report called decoding Codal's `LetterType`/`Category` codes "an early engineering task". That task is now done by reading Codal's own lookup endpoint. `https://search.codal.ir/api/search/v1/categories` returns `[{Code, Name, PublisherTypes:[{Code, Name, LetterTypes:[{Id, Name, Code}]}]}]`, and the numeric key is `Id` because the string `Code` is empty on every entry ([search.codal.ir categories](https://search.codal.ir/api/search/v1/categories); [codal-tsetmc category.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mohsenebrahimyir/codal-tsetmc/master/src/codal_tsetmc/download/codal/category.py)). The categories that matter for Boors are 1 (annual financial information), 2 (material disclosures), 3 (monthly performance), 5 (board), 6 (AGM invitations and decisions) and 7 (capital increases). Inside them, the LetterType IDs for the plan's §2.6 feed are 58 "گزارش فعالیت ماهانه" (form ن-۳۰), 6 "اطلاعات و صورتهای مالی میاندوره ای" (form ن-۱۰, used for annual statements too), 11 "افشای اطلاعات با اهمیت" (ن-۲۰), 16/17/18 AGM invitations, 20/21/22 AGM decisions (ن-۵۲ for 20) with 2020/2121/2222 as summaries, 30 "زمانبندی پرداخت سود" (ن-۱۳), 32 board activity report, 8 portfolio statement for investment companies, and the capital-increase chain 23 → 24 → 55/80/56 → 45 → 53 → 57 → 25 → 27 → 119 → 28 → 164 ([search.codal.ir categories](https://search.codal.ir/api/search/v1/categories)). Two things the plan assumed turn out not to be letter types: "گزارش تفسیری مدیریت" is published as an attachment under ن-10 letters and must be found by filtering `Attachment.aspx` titles, and the group A/B class of material disclosures is encoded in the Title of LetterType 11, for example `"افشای اطلاعات بااهمیت - (تحصیل سهام - گروه الف) منتهی به سال مالی ۱۴۰۶/۰۶/۳۱"`, with an EN DASH sometimes replacing the hyphen before "گروه الف" ([v2 q LetterType=11](https://search.codal.ir/api/search/v2/q?Audited=true&AuditorRef=-1&Category=-1&Childs=true&CompanyState=-1&CompanyType=-1&Consolidatable=true&IsNotAudited=false&Length=-1&LetterType=11&Mains=true&NotAudited=true&NotConsolidatable=true&PageNumber=1&Publisher=false&TracingNo=-1&search=true)). That distinction matters because group-A disclosures trigger unlimited-band reopenings (§2.3 and §6.3 of the plan).

The search API shape is confirmed live. `https://search.codal.ir/api/search/v2/q` takes `PageNumber, Symbol, LetterType, Category, Length ∈ {-1,3,6,9,12}, FromDate, ToDate, Audited, NotAudited, Childs, Mains, Consolidatable, NotConsolidatable, AuditorRef, PublisherType, CompanyState, CompanyType, TracingNo, Publisher` with `-1` meaning "no filter" ([codal-tsetmc query.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mohsenebrahimyir/codal-tsetmc/master/src/codal_tsetmc/download/codal/query.py); [codalpy query.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yghaderi/codalpy/master/codalpy/utils/query.py)). A LetterType=58 query on 17 October 2026 returned `{"Total": 99712, "Page": 4986, "Letters": [...]}`, each letter carrying `TracingNo, Symbol, CompanyName, Title, LetterCode, SentDateTime, PublishDateTime, HasHtml, HasExcel, HasPdf, HasXbrl, HasAttachment, Url, PdfUrl, ExcelUrl, XbrlUrl` and dates in Persian digits such as `"۱۴۰۵/۰۷/۲۵ ۱۴:۰۰:۲۹"`; the Excel link is `https://excel.codal.ir/service/Excel/GetAll/{LetterSerial}/0` and the `let=` query parameter in `Url` equals the LetterType Id ([v2 q LetterType=58](https://search.codal.ir/api/search/v2/q?Audited=true&AuditorRef=-1&Category=-1&Childs=true&CompanyState=-1&CompanyType=-1&Consolidatable=true&IsNotAudited=false&Length=-1&LetterType=58&Mains=true&NotAudited=true&NotConsolidatable=true&PageNumber=1&Publisher=false&TracingNo=-1&search=true)). Totals give a sense of volume: 138,570 ن-10 letters, 37,921 ن-20, 15,031 ن-52 and 14,952 ن-13 ([v2 q LetterType=6](https://search.codal.ir/api/search/v2/q?Audited=true&AuditorRef=-1&Category=-1&Childs=true&CompanyState=-1&CompanyType=-1&Consolidatable=true&IsNotAudited=false&Length=-1&LetterType=6&Mains=true&NotAudited=true&NotConsolidatable=true&PageNumber=1&Publisher=false&TracingNo=-1&search=true); [LetterType=20](https://search.codal.ir/api/search/v2/q?Audited=true&AuditorRef=-1&Category=-1&Childs=true&CompanyState=-1&CompanyType=-1&Consolidatable=true&IsNotAudited=false&Length=-1&LetterType=20&Mains=true&NotAudited=true&NotConsolidatable=true&PageNumber=1&Publisher=false&TracingNo=-1&search=true); [LetterType=30](https://search.codal.ir/api/search/v2/q?Audited=true&AuditorRef=-1&Category=-1&Childs=true&CompanyState=-1&CompanyType=-1&Consolidatable=true&IsNotAudited=false&Length=-1&LetterType=30&Mains=true&NotAudited=true&NotConsolidatable=true&PageNumber=1&Publisher=false&TracingNo=-1&search=true)). The ن-10 title patterns distinguish period and audit status in text: `"اطلاعات و صورت‌های مالی میاندوره‌ای  دوره ۶ ماهه منتهی به  ۱۴۰۵/۰۶/۳۱ (حسابرسی نشده)"` versus `"صورت‌های مالی  سال مالی منتهی به ۱۴۰۴/۱۲/۲۹ (حسابرسی شده)"`, with double spaces and an optional `"(اصلاحیه)"` suffix, so the plan's parser must normalise whitespace before regex matching.

The monthly activity report, the centrepiece of the plan's Phase 4, has a structure the plan can code against today. The letter page `Decision.aspx?LetterSerial=...&let=58` embeds `var datasource = {...};` whose top level carries `title_Fa` ("گزارش فعالیت ماهانه تولیدی - V4"), `type` (58), `period` (1), `periodEndToDate`, `yearEndToDate`, `isAudited` and `sheets[]`, each sheet holding `tables[]` with `aliasName` and `cells[]`; the manufacturing template's tables are 3464 `BuyRawMaterial`, 3465 `ProductionAndSales`, 3466 `Energy`, 3467 free-text corrections and 3640 `SourceUsesCurrency` ([codal.ir Decision.aspx sample](https://codal.ir/Reports/Decision.aspx?LetterSerial=8W0Ztnh839IwvvnHMZz1SQ%3d%3d&rt=0&let=58&ct=0&ft=-1)). Each cell carries `address`, `rowCode`, `columnCode`, `rowTypeName` (`FixedRow` for section headers, `CustomRow` for products, `NewRow` for blanks), `valueTypeName`, `dataTypeName`, `value` and, crucially, its own `periodEndToDate`. `ProductionAndSales` has column groups for cumulative-to-previous-month, corrections, corrected cumulative, the current month, cumulative including the current month and the same period last year, each with "تعداد تولید", "تعداد فروش", "نرخ فروش (ریال)" and "مبلغ فروش (میلیون ریال)". Because header cells carry the matching `periodEndToDate`, the robust way to split "current month vs cumulative vs prior year" is by that field rather than by header text. codalpy already does exactly this with the regex `var datasource = (.*?);` and typed `Sheet`/`Table`/`Cell` models ([codalpy codal.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yghaderi/codalpy/master/codalpy/codal.py); [codalpy models.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/yghaderi/codalpy/master/codalpy/models.py)). This reverses the plan's "Excel first, HTML tables as fallback" ordering for monthly reports: the HTML datasource is typed and period-tagged, whereas the Excel export is an untyped render of the same data. Only the manufacturing template was inspected; bank, leasing, service and investment-company templates have different table aliases and need their own golden files.

### TSETMC filter language: four official pages define the variables

The plan's §2.2 promises a filter language whose variables are "compatible with the TSETMC filter conventions". The conventions are now enumerated from TSETMC's own help pages on old.tsetmc.com. Current-session scalars are `(l18)` symbol, `(l30)` name, `(tno)` trade count, `(tvol)` volume, `(tval)` value, `(py)` yesterday, `(pf)` first, `(pmin)`, `(pmax)`, `(pl)` last, `(plc)`, `(plp)` last change and percent, `(pc)`, `(pcc)`, `(pcp)` close and its change, `(eps)`, `(pe)`, `(tmin)`/`(tmax)` the allowed band, `(z)` shares, `(mv)` market cap, `(bvol)` base volume, `(cs)` sector, `(buyop)` open interest and `(predtran)` fund NAV; order-book levels 1–5 are `pd#`, `zd#`, `qd#` (bid price, count, volume) and `po#`, `zo#`, `qo#` (ask) ([old.tsetmc.com filter help 3196](https://old.tsetmc.com/Site.aspx?ParTree=151715&LnkIdn=3196)). The history array `[ih][n]` indexes n days ago with `[ih][0]` being the previous trading day, fields `PClosing, PDrCotVal, ZTotTran, QTotTran5J, QTotCap, PriceMin, PriceMax, PriceYesterday, PriceFirst`, and `[ih].length` can be below 60 for new or suspended symbols ([old.tsetmc.com 3197](http://old.tsetmc.com/Site.aspx?ParTree=151715&LnkIdn=3197)). The client-type object exposes `(ct).Buy_CountI`, `Buy_CountN`, `Buy_I_Volume`, `Buy_N_Volume`, `Sell_CountI`, `Sell_CountN`, `Sell_I_Volume`, `Sell_N_Volume` ([old.tsetmc.com 3198](https://old.tsetmc.com/Site.aspx?ParTree=151715&LnkIdn=3198)). The statistics array `[is1]..[is90]` covers 3- and 12-month averages and ranks of value, volume, trade count, positive/negative/no-trade days, and individual/legal buyer and seller volumes and counts ([old.tsetmc.com 3199](https://old.tsetmc.com/Site.aspx?ParTree=151715&LnkIdn=3199)). Expressions are JavaScript with `&&`, `||`, comparison operators, `Math.*` and a code mode of the form `true==function(){ ... }()` ([bourse-trader.ir/tsetmc](https://bourse-trader.ir/tsetmc)). Common idioms include the suspicious-volume test `(tvol)>[is5] && (tvol)>2*[is6]` ([khanesarmaye stock-filter](https://khanesarmaye.com/stock-filter/)) and the buy-queue test `(pd1)==(tmax)&&(qd1)>0` ([bourseiness.com](https://www.bourseiness.com/23286/tsetmc-filters)). The `(ob1).Buy_Qty_1` style names some tutorials use do not appear in official help and should be treated as unverified. The design implication is that the plan's "safe expression language" should be a sandboxed JS-compatible evaluator exposing those exact names, with `[ih]`, `(ct)` and `[is]` loaded lazily, so that thousands of published community filters can be pasted in unchanged.

### Market watch wire format and instrument codes

The `GetMarketWatch` endpoint the first round flagged as "probably depends on parameters" is now pinned down. The working call is `cdn.tsetmc.com/api/ClosingPrice/GetMarketWatch?market=0&industrialGroup=&paperTypes[0]=1...paperTypes[8]=9&showTraded=false&withBestLimits=true&hEven=0&RefID=0`, where `paperTypes` 1–9 are 1 سهام, 2 فرابورس بازار پایه, 3 تسهیلات مسکن, 4 حق تقدم, 5 اوراق بدهی, 6 اختیار معامله, 7 آتی, 8 صندوق سرمایه گذاری, 9 بورس کالا per `StaticData/GetStaticData` ([5j9/tsetmc market_watch.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/5j9/tsetmc/master/tsetmc/market_watch.py); [cdn.tsetmc.com GetStaticData](https://cdn.tsetmc.com/api/StaticData/GetStaticData)). A live call with `paperTypes[0]=1` alone returned about 838 KB under the single key `marketwatch` ([cdn.tsetmc.com GetMarketWatch](https://cdn.tsetmc.com/api/ClosingPrice/GetMarketWatch?market=0&industrialGroup=&paperTypes[0]=1&showTraded=false&withBestLimits=true&hEven=0&RefID=0)), which at a 5-second cadence is a meaningful bandwidth cost on a 10 Gbit shared PaaS and a strong argument for using the `hEven`/`RefID` parameters for incremental polling, as the legacy `MarketWatchPlus.aspx?h=&r=` did. The compact keys, matched against `GetClosingPriceInfo` and `GetInstrumentInfo`, map as follows (inferred from value matching, not from documentation): `lva`=symbol, `lvc`=name, `pdv`=last, `pcl`=close, `py`=yesterday, `pf`=first, `pmn`/`pmx`=day low/high, `pMin`/`pMax`=static thresholds (وسپهر: 5080×0.97≈4930, ×1.03≈5230), `qtj`=volume, `qtc`=value, `ztt`=trade count, `bv`=base volume, `csv`=sector code, `ztd`=shares, `insID`=instrument ID, `pmd`/`pmo`=best bid/ask, `hEven`=last trade time, and `blDs[]` holds the five best limits with `n, qmd, zmd, pmd` (demand) and `pmo, zmo, qmo` (offer) plus `rid` for incremental polling. The companion endpoints are `ClientType/GetClientTypeAll` (key `clientTypeAllDto`) and `MarketData/GetInstValueAllInstAllParam` (key `instValueAllInstAllParam`) for the `[is]` statistics ([5j9/tsetmc market_watch.py](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/5j9/tsetmc/master/tsetmc/market_watch.py)).

## UX benchmark: table stakes are in Iran already, the 2026 AI layer is not

The global bar in 2025–26 is defined by TradingView, and its AI features are not prediction. The AI Screener (public beta, August 2026) maps a natural-language request to standard screener filters, picks a column set, sorts by the most relevant metric and shows an "Explanation" panel of why each filter was applied, with a monthly request balance displayed above the input ([FX News Group](https://fxnewsgroup.com/forex-news/platforms/tradingview-introduces-ai-screener/)). Perplexity Finance offers cited conversational research, a market summary, an earnings hub, a heatmap, brokerage linking and the promise that "every data point is traceable back to its original source" at Free, Pro $20/month and Max tiers ([Sid Saladi Substack](https://sidsaladi.substack.com/p/perplexity-finance-101-the-complete)), though reviewers find it "lacks depth for serious research" ([Techpoint Africa](https://techpoint.africa/guide/perplexity-finance-review/)). Professional desktops share a pattern: Quantower's unlimited workspaces, colour-linked panel groups and lockable layouts ([Quantower](https://quantower.com/interface-features)), Bloomberg's command line that is both command entry and search ([Bloomberg help PDF](https://datacenter.safe-frankfurt.de/documents/Bloomberg_help_page.pdf)), and Capital.com's 2026 redesign adding an assistant to search markets and features "without breaking focus" ([TradeInformer](https://tradeinformer.com/broker-news/capital-com-launches-newly-designed-app-with-ai-features)). Design sources agree that trading interfaces are "denser than anything a usability guide would approve, almost always dark, and they refresh continuously" ([AdminLTE blog](https://adminlte.io/blog/trading-dashboard-design-examples/)), that green/red is the standard and deviating "breaks user trust immediately", that red/green must never be the only encoding, that alerts should be configurable in two taps or fewer, and that AI should be surfaced in the context of a specific asset rather than as a separate layer ([Lollypop Design](https://lollypop.design/blog/2026/june/trading-app-design/)).

Pricing anchors are consistent across the global tier. TradingView plans run $12.95–$199.95 per month billed yearly, and the most-repeated complaints are unexpected renewals, hard refunds, features moved behind paywalls, a basic plan that went from real-time to a 10-minute delay, and a 45-second wait for an alert to register ([Strike.money review](https://www.strike.money/reviews/tradingview); [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tradingview.com?page=9)). Koyfin charges $39 (Plus) and $79 (Premium) per month ([ToolRadar](https://toolradar.com/tools/koyfin/pricing)); Unusual Whales $50–$120 per month with a free tier that delays options flow by two days ([FlashAlpha](https://flashalpha.com/articles/spotgamma-vs-unusual-whales-comparison)); Simply Wall St $10–$40 per month with end-of-day data only ([StockUnlock](https://stockunlock.com/simply-wall-st-review.html)); TrendSpider from about $52 per month annually and Trade Ideas Holly about $178 ([TradersPost blog](https://blog.traderspost.io/article/tradingview-ai-features-chart-copilot-documents-news)).

Agah's AsaTrader has 3 million installs and a 4.0 rating from 30,709 votes on Cafe Bazaar, with a recent review complaining that "for 20 days orders register exactly 5 seconds after the golden time" ([Cafe Bazaar](https://cafebazaar.ir/app/com.agah.asatrader)). Farabixo offers simple and advanced modes, a hot list by horizon, per-symbol price alerts and 1405 commissions of about 0.464% buy and 0.975% sell ([Khane Sarmaye](https://khanesarmaye.com/farabixo/)). The lone AI entrant, Sahmeto, does document-grounded Q&A over Codal, explicitly gives no buy/sell/hold recommendation, operates under the licence of an investment-advisory firm, and is free with pricing "not yet finalised" ([Sahmeto](https://sahmeto.com/); [Sahmeto pricing](https://sahmeto.com/pricing)). No Iranian product was found offering a TradingView-grade multi-chart workspace, an explainable NL screener, a command palette or documented dark mode, and Toman price lists for Rahavard 365, BourseView and the rest are not publicly indexed, so the only Iranian price anchors are Farabi's commissions, the first round's Tablokhani figure of 300,000 T, and Sahmeto's free trial. The recurring Iranian complaint themes are order lag at the pre-open "golden" second, outages at open and close with no user messaging (Farabi, Agah and Mofid all failed pre-open on one day, and an Agah outage ran about 12 hours) ([Nabz-e Bourse](https://nabzebourse.com/fa/news/15363/در-پیش-گشایش-پرتفوی-کاربران-کارگزاری-فارابی-پرید-|-صدای-سهامدار); [Tejarat News](https://tejaratnews.com/قطع-سامانه-معاملاتی-کارگزاری-آگاه)), and confusing cash flows.

The synthesis for Boors is therefore a three-layer target. Table stakes it must match: a market watch with custom columns, templates, export and formula filters; a tree/bubble/scatter market map; haghighi/hoghoughi panels and queue views; candlesticks with indicators, templates, adjusted/unadjusted toggles and corporate events on the chart; two-tap price alerts; a portfolio with average cost and P&L; a dense dark desktop UI with green-up/red-down; and a free trial with tiered plans. Differentiators nobody in Iran ships: an explainable natural-language screener that compiles to the filter language and shows the reason per filter with a monthly quota; AI summaries of Codal letters with jump-to-source and an event-typed corporate-news feed filtered by watchlist; multi-chart grids with colour-linked symbol groups and lockable workspaces; a Bloomberg-style command palette; a Lite/Pro toggle; a Snowflake-style visual fundamentals score; the same rule driving scan, alert and backtest; colour-blind-safe redundant encoding; and incident transparency with a status banner and degraded mode. Mistakes to avoid: moving real-time data or alerts behind a paywall after launch, slow alert registration, outages at open with no messaging, treemaps where exact comparison is needed, dual axes, abrupt UI changes to professional users (Bloomberg's UX team had to disguise a Launchpad revolution as evolution) ([Bloomberg UX blog](https://www.bloomberg.com/ux/2017/11/10/relaunching-launchpad-disguising-ux-revolution-within-evolution/)), and AI buy/sell "signals", which are both a trust and a regulatory risk. Most of this is already in the plan's §7; the additions are the command palette, multi-chart grids with linked groups, the Lite/Pro toggle, the explanation panel on the NL screener, and a public, stable Toman price list as a launch principle.

## Quant and rules: Mehr 1405 status, limit classes, fees, validation

### Rules in force as of 25 Mehr 1405

No decision to widen the ±3% band, introduce asymmetric limits or move to T+1 appeared in any source through 17 October 2026, and the SEO head reconfirmed in Ordibehesht 1405 that the TSE stock and rights band stays at ±3% ([Charisma Learning](https://learning.charisma.ir/beginning-investing/دامنه-نوسان-چیست/)). What the plan's single `price_limit` block misses is that limits are a per-instrument-class attribute: ±3% for stocks on TSE and the IFB first, second and innovation markets, ±3% for rights and equity ETFs, **±4% for leveraged equity funds**, ±3% for fixed-income and real-estate funds, **±10% for gold and silver funds on IME**, and ±3/±2/±1% for the IFB base market's yellow/orange/red boards; hitting the ceiling or floor does not halt trading, a queue forms ([Charisma Learning](https://learning.charisma.ir/beginning-investing/دامنه-نوسان-چیست/)). The second correction concerns base volume. The "one share" rule of Dey 1404 applies to the TSE and IFB main markets, where the trading-knot rules are also gone ([Charisma Learning](https://learning.charisma.ir/beginning-investing/دامنه-نوسان-چیست/)), but the IFB base market introduced a dynamic base-volume model in Mehr 1404 that cut the median base volume from about 4 million shares to one share while leaving the mean at about 1.73 million, lifted the fill rate from about 35% to 88%, and reduced locked symbol-days from 17.2% to 2.3%; a planned revision adds a "queue coefficient" (one-week average traded volume divided by average queue volume, bounded 0.5–1) so base volume can halve under heavy queues ([Vananews, 26 Shahrivar 1405](https://vananews.com/fa/news/666470/)). So the plan's `close_method: vwap` from 2025-12-22 is right for the main markets and wrong for the base market, and that traded-to-queue ratio is a ready-made fill-probability feature for the backtester.

Trading hours changed in Mehr 1405 for one instrument class. By TSE board resolution, from 18 Mehr 1405 (10 October 2026) the session for fixed-income, real-estate, land-and-building, project and fund-of-funds ETFs ends at 18:00, and IFB applied the same from 19 Mehr; stocks and equity funds still close at 12:30 with the TAL phase at 12:45–13:00 for first- and second-market symbols ([Shahrara News](https://shahraranews.ir/fa/news/447404/); [Agah Pedia on TAL](https://pedia.agah.com/article/trading-at-last)). The plan's §3.4 session and §3.3 polling tiers must therefore become per-instrument-class, and the live poller must keep running until 18:00 for those funds. Settlement remains T+2 per a general 1405 guide ([Khanesarmaye settlement guide](https://khanesarmaye.com/trade-settlement/)).

Halts have shifted from war damage to supervision. The market reopened on 19 May 2026 (29 Ordibehesht 1405) after about 80 days with 42 large symbols kept closed ([Eghtesadonline](https://www.eghtesadonline.com/fa/news/2138656/)); on reopening day 178 symbols reopened without limits because of group-A disclosures and 71 because of AGMs, a published list of 211 ([Taadol list of 211](https://www.taadolnewspaper.ir/fa/news/387885/)). By 3 Shahrivar 1405 (25 August 2026) the TSE CEO said "only two or three large companies" remained halted for war damage (Foolad Khuzestan, Fajr and Mobin petrochemicals), with a competing report naming Jam and Mobin ([Eghtesadonline, 3 Shahrivar 1405](https://www.eghtesadonline.com/fa/news/2159301/); [Shargh Daily](https://www.sharghdaily.com/بخش-اقتصادی-12/1123630-)). In Mehr 1405, six refinery symbols (شپنا، شبندر، شتران، شبریز، شراز، شاوان) were suspended for a transparency review with TSE declining to confirm trades in five of them over suspected manipulation ([Petrochemiha](https://petrochemiha.ir/اتفاق-مهم-در-گروه-پالایشی-بورس؛-۶-نماد-ت/)), and 19 symbols were halted on 25 Mehr including two held to 29 Mehr for suspected manipulation ([Boursepress 243320](https://boursepress.ir/news/243320/)). Material-information halts reopen within two business days via a discontinuous auction without a price limit ([Boursenews](https://www.boursenews.ir/fa/news/308138/)), and IFB publishes a live list of halted symbols ([IFB](https://www.ifb.ir/Issuers.aspx?id=4)). For the engine this means `day_status` needs a `supervisory_halt` reason distinct from `symbol_halted`, and the reopening gap must be modelled as one auction print rather than a run of ±3% days. The Stabilization Fund bought 4.894 hemat across 131 symbols in the week to 28 Tir 1405 and, with the Development Fund, supported the market with about 12.5 hemat ([Hamshahri Online](https://www.hamshahrionline.ir/news/1053399/)), which argues for a "state support" regime flag alongside the existing closure regimes.

Fees conflict between sources and the plan should stop hard-coding one total. One table gives brokerage 0.144% + exchange 0.155% + CSD 0.100% + tech 0.065% = 0.464% to buy and 0.964% to sell including the 0.5% tax, a 1.428% round trip on TSE and 1.408% on IFB ([Khanesarmaye fees](https://khanesarmaye.com/transaction-fees/)); another gives about 0.3712% buy and 0.8812% sell, roughly 1.25% ([Kian Digital](https://kiandigital.com/blog/کارمزد-معاملات-بورس-چقدر-است؟)). Margin interest is quoted at 24–36% per year for 1405 by a broker page, with no official circular found ([Khanesarmaye credit purchase](https://khanesarmaye.com/stock-credit-purchase/)).

### Methods: what the literature adds to the backtest lab

The most useful new empirical result is a 1405 Financial Research Journal paper in which a GA-tuned XGBoost predicts TEDPIX direction with about 84% accuracy on 1398–1403 data, and SHAP attributes the combined model's power to real and legal liquidity inflows, the gold price, RSI, volume, MACD, ROA and ROE, with the authors concluding the market is inefficient at weak and semi-strong levels ([jfr.ut.ac.ir article 103114](https://jfr.ut.ac.ir/article_103114.html)). That is index direction rather than a tradable cross-section, but it confirms the plan's §6.1 flow metrics and the practitioner lens of daily net real-money inflow and per-capita ratios ([YJC](https://www.yjc.ir/fa/news/9138398/)), and it says gold belongs in the feature set. Monthly activity reports also carry information: prices move in the direction of the news and volume rises around publication ([Journal of Accounting Knowledge](https://jak.uk.ac.ir/article_3125.html?lang=fa)), which validates the plan's Phase 5 event study and supplies the anchor dates via LetterType 58.

The A-share literature supplies the limit-hit template the plan needs. In emerging limit-constrained markets, daily momentum exists but its measured continuation falls by more than half when limit-hit observations are excluded ([AEA 2025](https://www.aeaweb.org/conference/2025/program/paper/dDyQH6K4)); Chinese investors overreact to up-limit events, erasing conventional momentum until up-limit days are dropped ([Economic Modelling](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0264999322001560)). Open-source A-share backtesters refuse fills at limit-up or limit-down opens and model next-open fills with T+1 and look-ahead checks ([quant-backtest](https://github.com/HongruiLi5j/quant-backtest)), and tradability filters exclude names suspended or hitting a limit on the next day ([MLF_2021_Final_Project](https://github.com/Harveydentsun/MLF_2021_Final_Project)); liquidity vanishes right after a limit hit, so naive backtests show fills that would not occur ([Gank Interview](https://www.gankinterview.cn/en/blog/a-shares-vs-us-stocks-interviewing-at-top-domestic-private-funds-high-flyerjiuku)). Because TSETMC exposes queue volumes, Boors can infer locks more reliably than the price-only Chinese method. For validation, purged cross-validation removes training observations whose label windows overlap the test set and adds an embargo buffer ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purged_cross-validation)); the 2026 "When Alpha Breaks" paper uses a LightGBM ranker on seven point-in-time features, a 90-trading-day embargo, per-date Spearman RankIC as the metric, and reports the 20-day RankIC collapsing from 0.072 in development to 0.010 out of sample, with an uncertainty gate that abstains 47% of the time ([arXiv 2603.13252](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.13252)). Practitioner repositories converge on a shallow LightGBM (depth 4, 15 leaves, heavy subsampling) regressing cross-sectional percentile rank under purged walk-forward CV, with Newey-West t-stats and Benjamini-Hochberg FDR control ([equity-xs-alpha](https://github.com/tejaspandya9598/equity-xs-alpha)). The plan's §6.3 and §2.14 should adopt these: embargo at least as long as the label horizon, DEV versus FINAL reporting, RankIC as the primary metric, and a tradability filter applied at the signal date. For covariance, Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage remains the default ([PyPortfolioOpt docs](https://pyportfolioopt.readthedocs.io/en/latest/RiskModels.html)), but halted names should be treated as missing rather than zero, because TSE halts end with ±20–40% auction gaps that the China "set to 0" shortcut understates.

The serving pipeline is documented end to end. Train LightGBM, export the booster, convert with onnxmltools `convert_lightgbm` using a `FloatTensorType` input and target opset 9 or 13 ([Microsoft Fabric ONNX overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/fabric/data-science/onnx-overview)), then load in .NET with ONNX Runtime 1.31. ML.NET's `ApplyOnnxModel` also works but requires output column names matching the ONNX graph ([Microsoft Learn API](https://learn.microsoft.com/dotnet/api/microsoft.ml.onnxcatalog.applyonnxmodel)); either way, export with ZipMap disabled so scores are a plain float tensor and inspect graph I/O names before wiring. Deep sequence models remain unsupported at monthly horizons: ARIMA and random forests remain hard to beat out of sample and transformer gains depend on evaluation design ([JRFM 19(3):203](https://www.mdpi.com/1911-8074/19/3/203)).

For the AI layer, the MELAC benchmark (41 models, 19 Persian datasets) changes the plan's fallback model. Average scores were GPT-4o 72.6, Gemini 2.0 Flash 70.7, Qwen3-32B 60.3, Gemma-3-27B 60.3, Aya-Expanse-32B 59.6, Hormoz-8B 50.5, Llama-3.1-8B 49.5 and Dorna2-Llama3.1-8B 47.7, below its own base model; the authors write that Persian-fine-tuned models "demonstrate notable performance limitations" ([arXiv 2508.00673](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.00673v1)). A 2026 SilkRoadNLP paper found Llama 3.1-8B's rejection accuracy fell from 73% to 30% after Persian adaptation into Dorna2 ([ACL Anthology 2026.silkroadnlp-1](https://aclanthology.org/2026.silkroadnlp-1.pdf)). The plan's "local model (Dorna2-8B via Ollama)" should become Qwen3-32B or Gemma-3-27B, with the caveat that no Persian financial benchmark or Codal extraction study exists, so accuracy on the 200-letter test in Phase 8 must be self-measured.

### Macro facts for the deflators and hurdles

The macro context sets the plan's denominators. The Statistical Centre of Iran reported Shahrivar 1405 point-to-point inflation of 89.8%, a 12-month rate of 73.6% and a monthly 4.2% ([YJC](https://www.yjc.ir/fa/news/9133044/)); the Central Bank, measuring an urban basket, reported 83.8% point-to-point, 68.4% for 12 months, goods at 118.1% and services at 50.9% ([Rahbord-e Moaser](https://rahbordemoaser.ir/fa/news/320052/)). The ~6-point gap confirms the plan's rule of labelling the CPI source. The exchange rate is a two-tier regime: the Exchange Centre (مرکز مبادله) remittance dollar sold at 181,641 toman on 25 Mehr 1405 ([Vananews 25 Mehr](https://vananews.com/fa/news/668506/)) while the free-market dollar was 264,800 ([Eghtesadonline 25 Mehr](https://www.eghtesadonline.com/fa/news/2169433/)), a gap of about 46%, and IME petrochemical base prices for the week to 12 Mehr were computed on the Exchange Centre rate of 168,181 toman ([Nabz-e Gheymat](https://nabzgheymat.ir/قیمت-پایه-محصولات-پتروشیمی-تا-۱۲-مهر-│/)). Exporter earnings models should therefore use the Exchange Centre rate lagged by the weekly IME cycle, and the free-official gap is itself a regime variable the plan's §2.8 does not yet carry. Fixed-income ETFs returned 34–41% over the trailing year (Pasargad 40.19%, Mahoor 39.40%, Onyx 38.00%) with some open-end funds at 54–58% ([Tabdeal Academy](https://tabdeal.org/academy/best-fixed-income-funds-1405/); [Rade.ir](https://www.rade.ir/investment-fund/)), which against ~90% inflation means real rates near −30% to −50%; a Sharpe ratio against that hurdle flatters any equity strategy, so the tear sheet should also report against gold-fund and dollar benchmarks. Gold funds are where retail money went: NAV passed 800 hemat in early Shahrivar, 911 hemat by 1 Mehr and 1,000 hemat by 10 Mehr on 10,724 billion toman of weekly retail inflow, with 77,748 billion toman of year-to-date inflow by 18 Mehr ([Atlas Eqtesad](https://atlaseqtesad.ir/114244/gold-funds-nav-record-record.html); [Jahan-e Sanat](https://jahanesanat.ir/ورود-11-همت-پول-حقیقی-به-صندوقهای-طلا/); [Shahrara 18 Mehr](https://shahraranews.ir/fa/news/451417/)). TEDPIX's rise from about 4 million at the May 2026 reopening to 8.46 million on 20 Mehr 1405 ([Asriran](https://www.asriran.com/fa/news/1198268/)) is of the same order as cumulative inflation plus the dollar move, which is the strongest possible argument for the plan's insistence on deflated returns.

## Conclusion

The second round converts the plan from a well-reasoned architecture into a buildable specification, and it does so mostly by confirmation: of the roughly thirty technology and design decisions in the plan, one is blocked (Advanced Charts), two are revised (PostgreSQL 18, a multilingual rather than Persian-tuned fallback model), and the rest are validated with concrete versions, licences and endpoints. The more consequential shift is in where complexity lives. The first round located the hard problem in data honesty (limits, closures, phantom rows); this round shows that the honesty problem is itself per-instrument-class and per-market: limits of 1 to 10%, base volume that is one share on the main boards but dynamic on the IFB base market, sessions ending at 12:30 for shares and 18:00 for income funds, and halts that now come from supervision rather than war and end in unlimited auctions. A single `rules.yaml` block cannot express this; the rules table needs an instrument-class dimension, and the backtester needs a tradability filter and a queue-conditioned fill model from day one rather than as a Phase 5 refinement.

Two findings should change how the project is sequenced rather than what it contains. First, the Codal monthly report's embedded `datasource` JSON and the enumerated LetterType IDs make the Phase 4 Codal work far more tractable than the "Excel first, HTML fallback" plan assumed, so the monthly-sales series and the event study it feeds could move earlier, perhaps overlapping Phase 3. Second, the UX benchmark shows that the differentiators nobody in Iran ships are not exotic: an explainable natural-language screener that compiles to the TSETMC-compatible filter language, Codal summaries with jump-to-source, a command palette and multi-chart workspaces. Each of those sits on infrastructure the plan already builds (the filter language, the Codal parser, the market-state diff engine), which means the "best possible" version of Boors is less a matter of adding features than of exposing the data layer's rigour through a few well-chosen interaction patterns, while keeping every AI output explainable, source-linked and free of buy/sell calls until a type-2 licence exists.
